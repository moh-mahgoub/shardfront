How to play

    

      

        
Touch

        
    
          
  • Tap a unit or building to select it.
    • 
          
  • With units selected, tap ground to move, tap an enemy to attack, tap shards to gather.
    • 
          
  • Double-tap a unit to select all of that type on screen.
    • 
          
  • Drag one finger: box-select in Select mode, pan in Camera mode (toggle at the left edge).
    • 
          
  • Two fingers always pan and pinch-zoom.
    • 
          
  • Group buttons 1-5: tap to select, tap again to jump there, long-press to assign.
    • 
          
  • Build: pick a structure, drag the ghost, then tap the ghost or the green tick.
    • 
          
  • Minimap: tap to jump, drag to pan.
    • 
        

      

      

        
Mouse & keyboard

        
    
          
  • Left-click / drag to select, right-click to move, attack, gather or set a rally point.
    • 
          
  • Wheel zooms, arrow keys or middle-drag pan.
    • 
          
  • A attack-move, M move, P patrol, S stop, H hold, B build, Esc cancel / pause.
    • 
          
  • 1-5 select group, Ctrl/Alt+1-5 assign. F3 debug overlay.
    • 
        

        
Goal

        
    
          
  • Workers mine shards. Supply buildings raise your unit cap.
    • 
          
  • Melee beats anti-armor, anti-armor beats armored, armored beats light units, ranged beats melee.
    • 
          
  • Destroy every enemy structure to win.
